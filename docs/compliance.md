# Checks and their scope

Checked 15 November 2026. The command identifies missing evidence for an operator to review. It does not certify compliance or determine a worker's competence.

| Rule | Check and basis |
|---|---|
| PURPOSE | Request has a recorded operational purpose. This is a local evidence control supporting purpose-limited personal information handling. |
| RETENTION_REVIEW | Missing or elapsed review dates need a human retention decision. New Zealand Privacy Act principle 9 limits retention to what remains necessary for a lawful purpose. The date is set by the organisation, not a statutory duration. |
| SAFETY_BRIEF | Safety-related requests need risks, worker input and a delivery method before local approval. These are local evidence fields supporting suitable training and worker engagement; filling them is not proof the training is adequate. |
| OWNER | An active person owns each approved request. This is an internal accountability rule, not a statutory role requirement. |

Sources: [NZ Privacy Commissioner, principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/); [WorkSafe, information, training, instruction and supervision](https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/managing-health-and-safety/businesses/general-requirements-for-workplaces/providing-information-training-instruction-or-supervision-for-workers/); [OAIC, APP 11](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information).

WorkSafe describes suitable and understandable training, competent trainers, worker engagement and review. It states that general training records are useful rather than universally required under that guidance. Special activities can have additional requirements. This desk covers the training brief, not individual qualifications or hazardous-substance training registers.

For Australian entities covered by the APPs, APP 11 includes protection and disposal or de-identification when information is no longer needed, with exceptions for Commonwealth records and legal retention requirements. A due review never deletes data. The operator decides applicability, lawful retention and legal holds, including copies in imports, audit history, backups and generated documents.

Project completion records delivered work, not regulatory clearance. Outcome comparisons are observations, not proof of causation. Capacity, budget and evidence gates are internal operating controls. Shared operation also needs authenticated access, tested backups, security monitoring and an agreed retention process.
